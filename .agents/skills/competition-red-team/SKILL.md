# Competition Red Team

Attack the case before submission. Classify findings as `FATAL`, `MAJOR`, or `MINOR`. Check fabricated-looking data, weak fieldwork, shallow tax reasoning, generic industry claims, incorrect/expired/inapplicable policy, arbitrary thresholds, correlation-as-causation, anomaly-as-illegality, missing false-positive/evidence checks, unreproducible amounts, cross-format number drift, AI/template language, unnecessary ML, leakage, and indefensible models. Produce the 30 most likely judge questions. Never soften a finding to help prose.
