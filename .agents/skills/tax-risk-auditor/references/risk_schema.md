# Required Risk Record

`rule_id`, `rule_name`, `tax_type`, `business_process`, `source_tables`, `source_fields`, `formula`, `calculated_value`, `threshold`, `threshold_basis`, `policy_basis`, `anomaly_reason`, `false_positive_checks`, `evidence_required`, `evidence_found`, `evidence_missing`, `final_status`, `risk_level`, `financial_impact`, `remediation`, `monitoring_rule`.

Allowed statuses: `OBSERVATION`, `ANOMALY`, `POTENTIAL_RISK`, `NEED_TAX_REVIEW`, `CONFIRMED_RISK`, `CLEARED`.
